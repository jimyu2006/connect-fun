# Connect-fun

Connect Fun is a relaxing and addictive board puzzle game.

Sign in easily with your Google or Facebook account. Only basic information (name and email) is used to identify login user.

Move fruits on the board and connect five or more identical ones in a row—horizontally, vertically, or diagonally—to clear them and score points. Earn bonus rewards for chain reactions and larger matches.

After each move, three new fruits appear randomly. Plan carefully—the game ends when the board is full!

Enjoy different themes, save your progress anytime, compete on leaderboards, and share the fun with friends.
